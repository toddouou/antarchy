How it works

One queen. Ten thousand ants. The whole Earth.

Antarchy is a single, persistent, planet-scale battlefield. No matches, no lobbies — just one living world where every painted pixel is contested territory.

01

Place your queen

Pick any spot on the real-world map — your hometown, a contested capital, the open ocean's edge — and plant your hive.

02

Deploy ant workers

Each worker follows Langton's-ant rules: flipping tiles, building highways, and emergently painting your colony's color across the grid.

03

Take territory

Tiles you paint are yours. Climb the live global leaderboard as your empire's footprint grows across countries and continents.

04

Besiege rival queens

Send brutes to break enemy lines and grind down rival queens. Level up, unlock shields, relocations, and heavier units.
